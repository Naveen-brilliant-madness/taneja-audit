What's
Holding You Back

8 Critical Gaps Found
🚫
No Custom Audiences — Zero Retargeting
Every campaign is targeting cold audiences from scratch. There are no website visitor audiences, no video viewer audiences, no past purchaser audiences, and no cart abandoner audiences being retargeted. This means you are paying full cold CPP for buyers who already know the brand and just need a nudge — the most expensive and least efficient way to run ads.
Critical Fix
🚫
No Lookalike Audiences Built
With 5,000+ purchases on record, Taneja has an extremely valuable dataset for building 1–3% Lookalike Audiences of buyers. A Lookalike of past purchasers finds people on Meta who share the same behaviour, demographics, and interest patterns as people who already bought. This consistently cuts CPP by 30–50% vs cold interest targeting. Currently this data is being completely wasted.
Critical Fix
⚠️
No Purchase List Uploaded to Meta
Taneja has an existing customer database — every past buyer's phone number and email can be uploaded to Meta as a Customer List Custom Audience. This enables: (1) direct remarketing to past buyers for new arrivals and offers, and (2) building a Lookalike Audience from verified purchasers, which is the highest-quality seed audience possible for Lookalike campaigns.
Critical Fix
⚠️
No Geographic Intelligence — Spending Everywhere Equally
All campaigns appear to target broadly without differentiating between high-purchasing cities and low-converting areas. Women with higher disposable income in Tier 1 cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai) have a significantly higher AOV and lower return rate for ethnic wear. There is no geographic bid adjustment or location exclusion happening currently.
High Priority
⚠️
All 22 Campaigns are OFF — No Always-On Campaign
Every single campaign in the account is currently turned off. There is no always-on evergreen campaign maintaining pixel data collection, keeping the account warm, and capturing daily demand. When campaigns are repeatedly turned on and off, the algorithm loses its learning and has to restart from scratch every time — resulting in higher CPP during the re-learning phase.
Critical Fix
⚠️
Website Has Conversion Rate Issues
The website (tanejaclothhouse.com) uses a Nushop builder — the banner images load as "BANNER_undefined__undefined" in the source code, meaning banner alt text and structured data are missing. The "50% Off" countdown timer is always running (it resets), which erodes trust with repeat visitors. No trust badges or payment icons are visible above the fold. All these reduce add-to-cart and checkout conversion rates.
High Priority
⚠️
Instagram Has 201k Followers — Not Being Retargeted
201,000 Instagram followers who engage with Taneja content are a massive warm audience — people who already love the brand and its products. None of them are being retargeted with ads. An Instagram Engager Custom Audience (people who liked, saved, commented, or DM'd) would have a CPP 40–60% lower than cold traffic because they already have brand affinity.
High Priority

Website Conversion
Audit

5 Issues Found
⚠️
Fake Countdown Timer — Resets Every Time
The "50% Off — 00H:26M:22S" countdown timer is not linked to a real end time. It resets on every page load. Customers who visit twice in the same day will see the same countdown — eroding trust and making the urgency feel dishonest. This is one of the fastest ways to lose a return visitor. Replace with real limited-time offers with genuine deadlines.
Fix Now
⚠️
Banner Images Have No Alt Text — SEO & Accessibility Loss
Every homepage banner loads as "BANNER_undefined__undefined" in the source code — meaning alt text is missing entirely. This hurts Google Image Search rankings (a significant traffic driver for fashion brands), and fails accessibility standards. Each banner should have descriptive alt text: "Pure Cotton Suit Set — Shop Ethnic Wear for Women."
SEO Fix
⚠️
No Trust Signals Above the Fold
First-time visitors from ads have no immediate reason to trust the website before scrolling. There are no visible: payment badges (Razorpay, UPI, COD available), delivery promises ("Free shipping on all orders"), return policy callout, or review/rating count. For a brand targeting customers who have never bought from Taneja before, this is a significant conversion barrier.
CRO Fix
⚠️
Discount Presentation May Train Customers to Wait
Products are shown with 55%–67% discounts by default at all times. While discounts drive impulse purchases, running permanent deep discounts trains returning customers to always expect them, reducing full-price purchase behaviour and making the "original price" feel fictional. Consider segmenting: show urgency discounts only for specific campaigns, not as the site's default state.
Strategy Fix
⚠️
Google Analytics & Pixel Events Not Verified
The site has a Facebook Pixel (ID: 907179631835692) installed but there is no visible confirmation that purchase, add-to-cart, and initiate-checkout events are firing correctly. If the pixel is misfiring, Meta is not getting accurate purchase signals — meaning campaign optimisation is flying blind. A pixel health check using Meta Events Manager should be the first technical action.
Critical Check
